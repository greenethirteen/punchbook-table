A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
take a seat.

Do guests need to install an app?

No. They scan the table QR code and the menu opens in their phone’s browser.

Can I try both sides of the experience?

Yes. Place a demo order in the Peppermint menu, then open the merchant dashboard to see how it appears to your team.

Are payments live in this demo?

Payments are simulated by default. Real card payments require merchant gateway setup. JustPay is currently a demonstration flow.

Is Punchbook ready for my restaurant?

This is an interactive prototype. A restaurant launch needs merchant onboarding, live payment setup, secure staff access and permanent order storage.
