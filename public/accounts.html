%

Days run midnight to midnight, Sri Lanka time. Live counts only real payments: simulated, sandbox and test-key payments are left out. Delivery fees stay with Punchbook to pay riders or PickMe, and Punchbook covers card fees. Cancelled and refunded orders are left out. Rider/PickMe costs are what riders enter on the rider page. Changing the commission doesn’t change payouts already marked sent. Bank file columns: name, account number, bank, branch, amount, reference. If your bank wants its own template, copy these across. Turn on “Alerts when closed” above to get a 9am alert listing payouts still to send.
