Connect a free Square Sandbox

  1. Create a free account at developer.squareup.com (no business or card needed).
  2. In the Developer Console, create an application, open it and switch to Sandbox. Copy the Sandbox access token.
  3. Add it to .env and restart the server:
    SQUARE_ACCESS_TOKEN=EAAA…your sandbox token…
SQUARE_ENVIRONMENT=sandbox
  4. To see orders in the POS: Developer Console → Sandbox test accounts → Open in Square Dashboard → Orders.
