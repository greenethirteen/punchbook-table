## Description
Skip the queue. Eat on time.

Punchbook lets you order ahead from restaurants near you. Browse the menu, pay on your phone and collect your food when it's ready, without waiting in line.

• Same prices as the counter. No app markups.
• Pick a pickup time: as soon as possible, or later today.
• Pay securely by card.
• Live updates from the kitchen, from "Order received" to "Ready at the counter".
• A pickup code to show at the counter, so the right order gets to you.
• No account needed. Your name and number are saved on your phone for next time.

Now live at Amra Leaf, Negombo, with more restaurants coming soon.
